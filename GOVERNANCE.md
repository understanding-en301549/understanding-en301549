# Governance

This project is run by a group of editors. There is no legal entity behind it. These documents are unofficial community documents. They carry no standing with ETSI, CEN, or CENELEC.

## Decisions

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Threshold | Decisions require a two-thirds majority of all editors. At seven editors, that is five approvals. |
| Response window | Editors get 14 days to respond to a decision. Silence counts as abstention; abstentions do not reduce the threshold. |
| Membership | Adding and removing editors uses the same two-thirds vote. |
| Content authority | Editors decide the content of the site. |

An editor's policy needs to be written. That policy is out of scope for this document.
