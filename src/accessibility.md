# Accessibility statement

We want this site to be accessible to as many people as possible.

## Known limits

This is a new site and has not yet had a formal accessibility audit. We do not
claim that it conforms to a particular accessibility standard. There may be
accessibility problems that we have not found yet.

Some guidance and supporting material is still being developed, so parts of
the site may be incomplete.

## Report an accessibility problem

If you find an accessibility problem, please
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/understanding-en301549/understanding-en301549/issues/new).
Describe the page, what you were trying to do, and the problem you encountered.

We will review reports and address problems where we can.

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies or analytics.
