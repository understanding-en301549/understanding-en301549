# About

Understanding EN 301 549 is an unofficial community project. It has no
affiliation or standing with ETSI, CEN, or CENELEC, and is not endorsed by
those organisations.

The project explains accessibility requirements in practical, plain English.
It does not replace the standard itself.

Editors and contributors participate in a personal capacity. Their work here
does not represent their employers or any standards organisation.
